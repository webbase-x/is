# Preparation chapter

Standalone chapter before unit 1, using the existing /P1/00-prerequisite/ route.

- Phasaphathi uploaded source: PDF pages 12–13, 44 original consonant illustrations. Images are extracted directly from embedded PDF images, ordered ก–ฮ; labels are native text.
- Thaksa Phasa workbook volume 1: printed pages 1–27. PDF pages 3–12 and 14–30; PDF pages 12 and 13 are duplicate scans of printed page 10. Original scans remain available via the source viewer, including marks already present in the supplied file.
- Practice groups follow the six preparation sets. Additional pages expose the vowel forms and four tone marks plus Thai numbers from printed pages 25–27.
- Native tracing guides are practice examples, not automated handwriting assessment. Drawing completion is a learner acknowledgement, not a correctness score. Ink is retained per page/symbol during the current page session, not submitted to the score database.
- Speech uses Thai device voices at rate 0.85. Each queued utterance controls its own highlight; changing pages cancels queued audio.
- Source artwork and workbook scans are separate assets; no reader data, songs, or existing chapter layout files are modified.

Run: npm ci --prefix P1/tests --ignore-scripts && npm test --prefix P1/tests

Consonants are displayed one card per page (44 cards), with taps for audio and horizontal swipes across the card for navigation. The preparation chapter now has 59 pages in total.
