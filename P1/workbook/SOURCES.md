Each unique worksheet is mapped to one gamified activity. All 150 worksheet pages are explicitly tagged in `book.json` with `gamified: true` and an `activityType`. Non-handwriting pages no longer use free drawing or typed completion; they are transformed into listening-choice, word-building, sentence-order, quiz, or page-specific interactive games. All source pages that explicitly require handwriting/tracing are tagged `handwriting` and rebuilt as touch/stylus tracing activities. There are 19 handwriting/tracing activities after source-page review: the final handwriting page of each chapter plus seven embedded Thai-numeral and vowel-tracing pages in Unit 3. The final handwriting scan placeholders for Units 11–12 are empty in this repository; their rebuilt tracing text is recovered from the corresponding lesson sources (`อายจัง อายจัง` and the 12-line `ปีใหม่ไทย`) rather than fabricating scan content. Progress remains device-local and scoped to the current student/teacher selection. Correct game completion or handwriting threshold unlocks the next activity, followed by the existing review and game.
